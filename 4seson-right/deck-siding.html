Premium Outdoor Decks and Siding Systems in Washington

Your home's exterior serves two vital functions: it defines your property's visual personality and acts as the primary barrier against the elements. In the Pacific Northwest, where damp weather, heavy rain, and seasonal temperature swings are common, choosing high-performance deck and siding materials is essential. Faded, cracked, or rotting siding allows moisture to penetrate structural framing, leading to toxic mold and costly structural repairs. Faded or splintering outdoor decks represent not only an eyesore but also a safety hazard. At 4 Seasons Right Services Inc, we design and build gorgeous outdoor living spaces and install elite weatherproof siding systems that protect your home while enhancing curb appeal.

A custom deck is the perfect extension of your home's living space. Whether you envision a sprawling multi-level deck for hosting weekend summer barbecues or a cozy, private retreat to enjoy quiet mornings, our outdoor design specialists will bring your vision to life. We work with a wide range of materials, including classic red cedar, pressure-treated lumber, and modern wood-plastic composites like Trex and TimberTech.

Why Homeowners Prefer Composite Decking Systems

Traditional wood decks offer timeless warmth and natural beauty, but they require periodic power washing, sanding, staining, and sealing to stay rot-free. For homeowners seeking a maintenance-free outdoor lifestyle, composite decking is the ideal solution. Built from recycled wood fibers and dense plastics, composite deck boards do not splinter, crack, warp, or fade.

We install composite decking using hidden fastening systems that leave a clean surface free of exposed screw heads. Our deck structures are built with pressure-treated lumber, concrete footings, robust joists, and sturdy ledger boards that exceed local building codes. We also customize your deck with premium railings, integrated LED stair lighting, and multi-tone border accents for a truly custom finish.

Elite James Hardie Fiber Cement Siding Installation

When it comes to siding, James Hardie fiber cement siding is the gold standard for durability and protection. Made from a blend of cellulose fibers, sand, and cement, fiber cement is fire-resistant, rot-proof, and impervious to pests like woodpeckers and termites. It replicates the classic look of real wood siding but does not rot, warp, or require constant repainting.

Proper siding installation requires deep expertise. Our siding crews begin by stripping away damaged siding, inspecting structural sheathing, repairing rot, and wrapping your home in a high-performance weather-resistant house barrier. This ensures that moisture is kept out while allowing water vapor to escape, preserving your home's structural health. We then install your new siding with absolute precision, utilizing premium flashing and caulking to seal every seam, window frame, and corner trim.
