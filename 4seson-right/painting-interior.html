Comprehensive Guide to Premium Interior Painting & Cabinet Refinishing

The interior paint of a house defines its identity, establishes its ambiance, and directly impacts the feeling of spaciousness. At 4 Seasons Right Services Inc, we treat interior painting as a fine art form rather than a routine chore. Serving residential and commercial clients across Washington, from the residential neighborhoods of Kirkland and Redmond to high-end estates in Bellevue, we bring seasoned craftsmanship and high-grade materials to every single room. We realize that the key to an extraordinary paint job lies 90% in meticulous preparation and 10% in flawless application.

We start every interior painting project with thorough surface protection and preparation. Before a single paint can is opened, our painters systematically seal your furniture, mask baseboards, sand imperfections, patch drywall dings, and fill trim gaps with premium caulking. This exhaustive preparation guarantees that the final coats of paint adhere perfectly, showing laser-straight cut lines where the walls meet the ceilings and baseboards.

Precision Cabinet Spraying & Trim Enameling

One of our most popular high-end services is professional kitchen cabinet spraying and refinishing. Replacing kitchen cabinets is an incredibly expensive project. By choosing our specialized cabinet spraying, you can completely transform your kitchen at a fraction of the cost. We remove all cabinet doors and drawer fronts, bring them to our controlled spray shop, clean away oils and cooking greases, sand them smooth, apply a specialized bonding primer, and spray-apply multiple coats of durable urethane acrylic enamel. The result is a smooth, factory-like satin finish that is exceptionally durable, washable, and beautiful.

Our trim specialists utilize premium brush-and-roll or fine-finish spray techniques to coat crown moldings, wainscoting, door frames, and window sills. We exclusively recommend Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore low-VOC, water-based enamel paints. These products offer superior yellowing resistance, scrubbability, and dry without emitting toxic chemical odors, keeping your family safe and healthy.

Our Systemized Interior Painting Process

We maintain absolute visual perfection by adhering to a scheduled process of milestones:

01. Setup & Surface Protection

We move lightweight furniture, cover remaining items with heavy plastics, and protect carpets and hardwoods with canvas drop cloths.

02. Drywall Prep & Caulking

We patch all cracks, sand wall textures, fill nail holes, and caulk all trim joints to guarantee seamless, airtight transitions.

03. High-Bond Sealer-Primer

We apply premium high-hide primers over raw sheetrock or patched areas to ensure perfect, streak-free paint absorption.

04. Double Premium Topcoats

We apply two full coats of premium Sherwin-Williams paint, completing the project with crisp margins and a detailed cleanup.
