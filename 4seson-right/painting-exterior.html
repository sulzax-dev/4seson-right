Ultimate Guide to Weatherproof Exterior Painting & Wood Staining

Western Washington is famous for its beautiful forests, but our damp climates, moss growth, and seasonal temperature swings present a massive threat to wood siding, trim boards, and outdoor structures. Over time, moisture gets trapped inside raw siding, leading to expensive dry rot, fungal spores, and structural instability. At 4 Seasons Right Services Inc, we understand that exterior painting is your home's first line of defense. Our professional painters provide a comprehensive, multi-phase system that seals your home from top to bottom, using high-performance paints and sealants.

A premium exterior paint job relies on thorough preparation. We never spray over dirty, wet, or flaking siding. Our process begins with a meticulous low-pressure wash to remove accumulated dirt, pollen, and moss spores without damaging the wood fibers. Once dry, we scrape loose flakes, sand rough grain edges, and seal all window borders, door frames, and siding butt-joints with a premium, high-stretch elastomeric caulking that expands and contracts with shifting weather.

High-Bond Exterior Paints & Cedar Siding Restoration

Whether your property features traditional cedar shakes, classic vinyl, rustic board-and-batten, brick masonry, or modern James Hardie fiber cement panels, we utilize specialized primers and coatings designed for that specific material. For wood surfaces, we apply penetrating mildewcide-treated primers to block tannin bleeding and provide a durable bond for the finish coats.

For final coatings, we exclusively utilize ultra-premium, heavy-bodied exterior paints like Sherwin-Williams Emerald Exterior Acrylic latex or Benjamin Moore Aura. These formulations represent the pinnacle of outdoor paint technology, featuring superior UV blocking to prevent fading, outstanding elasticity to resist cracking, and a hydrophobic barrier that repels water while allowing moisture vapor to escape.

Meticulous Prep and Coating Phases

We maintain absolute consistency and durable results by executing a rigorous four-step checklist:

01. Environmental Washing & Treatment

We wash all surfaces using eco-friendly cleaning agents to neutralize embedded mold and mildew, rinsing away years of grime.

02. Scraping & Sanding

We scrape loose paint flakes, sand wood ridges smooth, and replace any trim or siding that shows signs of dry rot.

03. Heavy-Duty Sealing

We fill nail holes, caulk all joints, seams, and window perimeters to block water leaks and drafts completely.

04. Double Spray-and-Backroll Coating

We spray-apply paint and follow behind with hand-rollers to push the paint deep into the wood pores for a thicker, uniform protective layer.
